Framing reset in effect throughout: QUORUM (`firmware/QUORUM/QUORUM.ino`) is field evidence, failure taxonomy, and comparison baseline only — not the behavioral standard. Matching QUORUM's acceptance ordering, recovery triggers, or NO_QUORUM behavior is evidence *against* a candidate, not for it. The three-way disposition taxonomy (Artifact / Expected credible passage / Credible contradiction) replaces QUORUM's two-way accept/reject and is the design constraint Part 1's dispositions are judged against.

**Tool identity, stated plainly:** `tools/templates_replay_spec.py` (implements `docs/TEMPLATES_REPLAY_DESIGN_SPEC.md`) is the raw-SAMPLE experimental candidate this document evaluates — it reads exclusively `phys_*`/`ctl_*` SAMPLE fields as input, never QUORUM's own decisions. `tools/templates_replay.py` is Sam's separate, independently-built QUORUM-event-summary baseline — it reads QUORUM's own `dec_*` DECISION-record interpretations as input and implements only a two-way taxonomy. These are two different designs, not variants of one tool, and **neither is firmware-approved or canonical**. Every citation below that references "the classifier" or "the candidate" means `templates_replay_spec.py` unless explicitly marked otherwise.

**Verdict:** per `docs/TEMPLATES_REPLAY_RESULTS_REPORT.md` §12, the raw-sample candidate is **rejected as currently tuned**, pending correction of contradiction-ring accumulation (Part 2, 2.3/2.8) and same-polarity omission masking (Finding 10, Part 2 2.1). ## Part 1 — Original 18 Doctrine Findings, Re-Disposed Against Replay Evidence

Each finding: doctrine hook / evidence (condensed from the original review), attack type (does it attack the doctrine's own text, or only an underspecified implementation of it), proposed resolution as informed by this session's replay, a replay test that could falsify that resolution, and disposition.

### Passage acquisition (§1)

**1. PWM is a single open-instant sample; a passage can outlast the ramp it opened in**
- *Evidence:* QUORUM's `evStartPwmActual`/`evStartPwmCommanded` captured once, at OPEN, never again; ramps run 700ms, genuine excursions run 0.5–1.2s.
- *Attack type:* Implementation underspecification — doctrine names PWM as evidence without specifying sampling cadence.
- *Resolution as built:* `templates_replay_spec.py`'s contradiction-observation contract stores `pwm_actual_at_open`, `pwm_at_peak`, `pwm_at_close` as three distinct fields (confirmed in PART 2 finding "Only 2 of ~13 recorded observation fields..."), closing the single-sample gap for the recovery-observation path. Whether the *primary-position* evidence record (not just the contradiction-ring record) carries the same three-point sampling was not independently re-checked this session.
- *Falsifying replay test:* Confirm `evidence_pwm_for_gate`/primary-passage evidence fields on an `EXPECTED_ADVANCE` row spanning a documented ramp window (e.g. a station-departure marker inside `STATION_UP_STEP_MS`) carry open/peak/close PWM that materially disagree; if they collapse to one value, the gap persists on the primary-position side even though it's closed on the recovery side.
- *Disposition:* **Revise** — implemented for Category-3 evidence, unconfirmed for Category-2.

**2. The reject-floor margin shrinks with speed and is unvalidated at QUORUM's own reference regime**
- *Evidence:* PWM40→PWM90 width drops ~2.97x for only 2.25x PWM increase; no capture in the original review exceeded PWM90; linear extrapolation to PWM255 left ~10–12ms margin above `EVENT_FLOOR_MS`=40ms against a 20–22ms documented reject population.
- *Attack type:* Doctrine itself — Consequence #10 ("select thresholds only from evidence") is violated by construction if no high-PWM evidence exists to select from.
- *Resolution as built:* PART 2's `PWM_VALIDATED_CEILING`=130 finding directly measured this in the replay phase: max `ctl_pwm_actual` across all four assigned captures is 90 (Toby, Otto CCW) or 120 (Otto CW, Otto change1) — never within 10 counts of 130, and QUORUM's own PWM255 reference point remains completely unreached. The `pwm_regime_unvalidated` diagnostic flag exists in code but has never fired on real data and has zero unit-test coverage forcing it to fire synthetically.
- *Falsifying replay test:* Feed a synthetic candidate with `pwm_for_gate`>130 directly to the `Replay` class (unit-level, bypassing captures) and confirm the flag fires and the event still receives ordinary disposition treatment; separately, add `max_pwm_for_gate_observed_this_run` to `report.json` so future real captures at higher PWM are visible without manual scanning.
- *Disposition:* **Unresolved** — finding stands essentially unchanged after the full replay phase; no capture available to this task reaches the regime the finding is about.

**3. The static morphology bar hasn't been checked for PWM-dependent bite on genuine passages**
- *Evidence:* `min_duration_ms`/`min_abs_flux` applied identically across regimes; REJECT_SPIKE rate gap 68.1%→92.5% between PWM40/PWM90 while genuine width differs ~3x and peak flux only modestly.
- *Attack type:* Implementation underspecification — doctrine's own Consequence #6 normalization discipline was never extended to the reject-rate breakdown itself.
- *Resolution as built:* `DURATION_FLOOR_MS`/`FLUX_FLOOR` remain fixed constants in `templates_replay_spec.py` (not PWM-scaled). No replay item in this session produced a duration/flux-vs-PWM histogram of *rejected* candidates the way Finding 3 asks for; the closest available evidence is PART 2's confirmation that `ARTIFACT_DURATION_FLOOR` dominates Otto CW's artifact population (301 of 323) without a PWM-regime breakdown.
- *Falsifying replay test:* Bucket every `ARTIFACT_DURATION_FLOOR`/`ARTIFACT_FLUX_FLOOR` disposition across all five real sessions by `pwm_for_gate` at time of rejection and check whether the high-PWM bucket's near-floor density resembles the low-PWM bucket's; a high-PWM cluster hugging the floor (rather than sitting with the sub-10ms/40-count post-stall population) would falsify the assumption that the fixed floor is PWM-invariant.
- *Disposition:* **Unresolved** — no new evidence gathered this session speaks directly to this finding.

**4 & 7. QUORUM's LOW_PWM path drops the arrival gate entirely, exactly where artifact rate is highest / "where appropriate" leaves low-PWM optional but every station departure passes through it**
- *Evidence:* `GATE_LOW_PWM_FLOOR`=40 in QUORUM; below it, `acceptEvent()` fires unconditionally with zero timing-conservation check. Grillers capture (PWM40 stall, 75s of post-stall narrow spikes) is the field evidence this exists to address.
- *Attack type:* Both — the doctrine's "where appropriate" qualifier (finding 7) is a doctrine-text gap; finding 4 is a concrete implementation instance of it.
- *Resolution as built:* The design spec's §5.4/§5.7 PWM-level (not edge-triggered) interval invalidation and `SUSPEND_PWM_FLOOR`=20 acquisition suppression are the intended TEMPLATES-side closure, and the design doc explicitly claims this "directly closes Findings 4 and 7." **This claim is unvalidated by any replay evidence gathered this session.** PART 2's Grillers finding establishes the purpose-built capture for exactly this scenario (`tools/manifests/grillers.json`, `pwm40_run.json`) was never run against `templates_replay_spec.py` by any of the seven replay items, and independently reproducing the attempt in this fact-check found it fails outright: the manifest's `operator_confirmed_start` key doesn't match what `Manifest.__init__` requires, and even after patching that, the underlying capture's CSV schema (`phys_ch0_raw`/`phys_t_us`) is incompatible with `_on_sample`'s required `t_ms`/`phys_raw`/`phys_baseline` fields — it silently emits zero candidate rows. What *was* validated: Toby's real PWM=0 station dwells correctly triggered `NO_PREV_INVALIDATED` and the first post-dwell marker passed clean map validation (`reversal_mid_interval_and_station_stop_restart`); but PWM=0 dwell is architecturally distinct from a sustained nonzero-low-PWM stall (Grillers), and the design doc's own text says the level-based `SUSPEND_PWM_FLOOR`=20 mechanism is "broader by construction" than QUORUM's edge-triggered fix specifically *because* it should cover the nonzero-stall case QUORUM's fix misses — a claim resting entirely on code reasoning, never exercised against Grillers data.
- *Falsifying replay test:* Build the schema adapter needed to run `templates_replay_spec.py` against `grillers.json`/`pwm40_run.json` (map `phys_ch0_raw`/`phys_t_us`→`phys_raw`/`t_ms`, or extend `qt_decode.py`'s older-format path) and report the disposition the sustained-stall region actually receives. If `SUSPEND_PWM_FLOOR`=20 fails to suppress the post-stall narrow-spike population, or a genuine broad passage during the stall is wrongly suppressed rather than flagged, the claimed closure is false.
- *Disposition:* **Unresolved** — the design *reasoning* for closure exists and is more defensible than QUORUM's own fix, but the specific field evidence this finding was built from has never been run through the candidate, and the schema incompatibility means it currently *cannot* be run without additional tooling work.

### Physical arrival gate (§2)

**5. The arrival-gate clock has no defined reset event, and QUORUM's own fix doesn't cover a stall**
- *Evidence:* QUORUM's `invalidatePreviousAcceptedDt()` fires only on a nonzero→zero PWM edge; Grillers' PWM stays nonzero (pegged at 40) throughout its documented 75s post-stall window, so the edge never fires in exactly the scenario it exists for.
- *Attack type:* Implementation underspecification — doctrine's §2 language never states what resets the clock.
- *Resolution as built and validated:* §5.7's stale-interval invalidation was directly exercised on real data: Toby's ~29 genuine ST_DWELL cycles (PWM held at exactly 0 for ~15,010ms, confirmed against QUORUM's own `DWELL_MS`=15000 constant) each correctly discarded the pre-dwell interval reference (`NO_PREV_INVALIDATED`) and the first post-dwell marker passed full map validation as `EXPECTED_ADVANCE` (`reversal_mid_interval_and_station_stop_restart`). This is real, positive replay confirmation for the PWM=0 dwell case specifically.
- *Falsifying replay test:* Same as Finding 4/7's — a Grillers-schema-adapted replay showing whether the sustained-nonzero-PWM stall case (not covered by the validated dwell case) also correctly resets the clock, or silently reproduces QUORUM's own gap.
- *Disposition:* **Revise** — resolved and replay-confirmed for the PWM=0 dwell case; the harder nonzero-stall case this finding was actually built around remains unvalidated (same root gap as 4/7).

**6. Reversal makes the "next" marker the one just left, at near-zero distance — undefined behavior**
- *Evidence:* QUORUM's `applyDirection()` fixes odometer bookkeeping only, then forces the first post-reversal event through the unconditional-accept `NO_PREV` branch — no polarity check.
- *Attack type:* Doctrine itself — §2's spacing-justified bound assumes full marker-spacing distance to the next marker, an assumption reversal violates by construction.
- *Resolution as built and validated:* Directly, positively confirmed on real anchored data. `otto_change1_run1_3E6A88F1`: the operator-anchored mid-run CW→CCW reversal (live-detected off `ctl_dir` 7,592ms before the operator's own anchor text logged it) triggered a single-shot `REVERSAL_EXEMPT` state that suspends only the timing floor — full artifact rejection and map-polarity validation still ran. The very reversal-adjacent candidate this creates a genuine risk for was correctly disposed `CREDIBLE_CONTRADICTION` (polarity mismatch caught), not blindly accepted; the next candidate 2.5s later passed cleanly as `EXPECTED_ADVANCE`. This is exactly the improvement over QUORUM's `NO_PREV`-unconditional-accept the finding calls for, confirmed on a fully anchor-bracketed run.
- *Falsifying replay test:* Already partially executed — a reversal-adjacent candidate that were falsely admitted as `EXPECTED_ADVANCE` despite wrong polarity would falsify this; none occurred in the one available real anchored reversal. A second independent reversal capture (none of the other three assigned files contains one) would strengthen confidence beyond n=1.
- *Disposition:* **Replace** — QUORUM's ordering deliberately not reproduced; TEMPLATES' `REVERSAL_EXEMPT` mechanism is a genuine, replay-validated departure and resolves the finding, on the one real instance available.

**8. A single global max-credible-speed bound is safe against over-rejection but nearly toothless at low speed**
- *Evidence:* One blanket bound (spacing / max_credible_speed at PWM255) is safe against false rejection everywhere but weak against false admission precisely where field failures concentrate (2_13 Grillers crawl, Otto's sub-40ms population).
- *Attack type:* Doctrine itself — never specifies whether the bound must be phase/context-aware.
- *Resolution as built and validated:* Partially closed by a *different* new mechanism than the one the finding proposed. `hard_ms` (the absolute-impossibility bound) is still evaluated unconditionally at PWM255 on every candidate (confirmed in PART 2's PWM-ceiling finding — this bound is the one always-active check, never PWM-scoped). But a new, independent `MAX_OPEN_MS`=3000ms excessive-open ceiling catches the specific baseline-tracking-corruption failure mode the finding is concerned about: `baseline_shift_and_transport_gap_adversarial_tests` found a real severe raw/baseline divergence during a deceleration-to-near-stall in Otto CW (the same failure class as the doctrine's own Grillers/2_13-crawl narrative) and confirmed the classifier force-closed it as `ARTIFACT_EXCESSIVE_OPEN` with zero ring insertion — QUORUM's own detector opened an equivalent event at the identical instant and never closed it in the visible window.
- *Falsifying replay test:* A genuine crawl-regime passage (real magnet transit, not baseline corruption) lasting >3000ms would be wrongly discarded by `MAX_OPEN_MS` as `ARTIFACT_EXCESSIVE_OPEN` — the Grillers-schema gap (4/7/5) again blocks testing this directly, since Grillers documents genuine passages stretching toward 4 seconds under real load.
- *Disposition:* **Revise** — the low-speed permissiveness of the PWM255-referenced `hard_ms` bound itself is unchanged and unresolved, but a structurally new, replay-confirmed mechanism (`MAX_OPEN_MS`) closes the specific baseline-corruption failure mode the finding's field evidence was drawn from.

**10. Same-polarity companions are acknowledged but never characterized — and they defeat the map-polarity backstop entirely**
- *Evidence:* No count/width/amplitude given for the same-polarity population in the doctrine text; QUORUM's real map contains consecutive same-polarity runs, so the danger is not hypothetical.
- *Attack type:* Doctrine itself — names the danger, supplies zero evidence to bound it, and the map-polarity backstop is structurally blind to exactly this case.
- *Resolution as built and validated:* **Confirmed as the single most load-bearing surviving danger in the entire exercise.** `omission_recovery_toby_case1_single_and_case2_multi` reproduced this finding concretely, on real data, on Toby's *cleanest* capture: deliberately constructed 1-, 2-, and 3-consecutive-marker omissions were silently and completely absorbed — zero `CREDIBLE_CONTRADICTION`, zero recovery event, zero liveness warning — whenever a coincidental same-polarity feature (either the map's own same-polarity run, or a previously-uncharacterized real near-duplicate same-polarity bump in the raw waveform) happened to sit in the omitted span. This reproduced on 3 of 4 tested omission counts. Both of the tool's own post-hoc audits (`audit_false_inclusion`, `audit_ring_contamination`) report clean in every one of these cases, because neither checks physical-position continuity — a design that passes its own audits is still silently wrong here. This is finding #10 made concrete, at scale, on real clean-control data, not a contaminated adversarial capture.
- *Falsifying replay test:* Already run and failed to falsify the danger — it is confirmed present. A test that *would* meaningfully narrow it: characterize the full same-polarity companion population (count/width/amplitude/time-since-primary) the way the opposite-polarity population is already characterized in the doctrine, across all five real sessions, to determine whether the danger is rare-but-catastrophic or a routine background rate.
- *Disposition:* **Unresolved / critical** — this is the one finding this session's replay phase most directly validated as a live, unmitigated risk in the shipped candidate. The three-way taxonomy's Category-3 mechanism cannot protect against this class of error by construction, since a same-polarity coincidence is classified `EXPECTED_ADVANCE` (Category 2), never reaches the contradiction ring at all, and there is currently no independent physical-plausibility signal (only 1-bit polarity) to catch it.

### Recovery (§4)

**14. The recovery trigger is disagreement-driven, not silence-driven — pure omission cannot wake it**
- *Evidence:* QUORUM's `missStreak`/`markersSinceConfirmed` only increment inside `acceptEvent()`, so a silently-discarded valid magnet can never touch either counter; original proposal called for an elapsed-distance/time liveness check independent of disagreement counting.
- *Attack type:* Doctrine itself — the omission-blindness is structural to QUORUM's counters, and the doctrine's Consequence #4 only demands demonstrating recoverability for a single omitted marker, not a multi-marker silent gap.
- *Resolution as built and validated:* This is the finding the operator's brief converts into the two explicitly required, distinct mechanisms — (a) event-driven hypothesis testing on the next arrival, (b) silence-driven travel-without-landmarks confidence/timeout independent of arrivals — and both are implemented and independently confirmed firing on real data. Mechanism (a): `case3_credible_contradiction`, `otto_cw_20260824_worst_contamination`. Mechanism (b), §7b liveness: `liveness_timeout_and_pulse_storm_admission` confirms both a graduated stage-1 warning (fired repeatedly across all three real full-session captures) and a genuine stage-2 `LIVENESS_TIMEOUT` safe-stop (Otto CCW, t=976144, elapsed=477634ms, independently verified against raw PWM samples as genuine sustained motion — 86.2% PWM>20 over the window, not a dwell). Both mechanisms confirmed structurally independent — liveness never advances position from PWM/dead-reckoning, only warns or stops, matching the operator's explicit prohibition. PART 2 identifies real calibration defects in mechanism (b) specifically (see Part 2, liveness_false_trigger cluster) but the mechanism's *existence and independence* — the actual doctrine gap — is resolved.
- *Falsifying replay test:* Already run for both mechanisms on real data across five sessions. Remaining open question is calibration (PART 2), not existence.
- *Disposition:* **Replace** — QUORUM's structural blindness is not reproduced; both required, distinct mechanisms exist and fire independently on real data. PART 2's liveness-calibration findings (velocity-model reuse, N_LIVENESS margin) attach to this finding's *implementation quality*, not its resolution status.

**15. The recovery window's fixed span is never checked against the doctrine's own increased omission rate**
- *Evidence:* QUORUM's `QUORUM_OFFSETS`={-1..+4}/`QUORUM_MAX`=12 represent at most 4 consecutive omitted markers before the true position falls outside the candidate set; sized for a less-conservative admission regime than the doctrine mandates.
- *Attack type:* Doctrine itself — connects candidate-window sizing to the precision-over-recall policy only in principle, never operationalized.
- *Resolution as built:* `MAX_OMITTED` and `CONTRADICTION_RING_SIZE`=12 are explicitly, deliberately inherited from QUORUM's own `QUORUM_MAX`/`QUORUM_OFFSETS` — confirmed in-code as "flagged for revalidation" and independently cross-checked against QUORUM's own onboard STATUS counter reporting `quorum(trigger=3 margin=2 max=12 cand=6)` on the same Otto CW capture. This is exactly the un-re-derived inheritance the finding warned against, now confirmed present by direct code citation rather than inferred. Replay evidence on whether 12 is *enough*: mixed and concerning in the opposite direction from the finding's original concern — real runs are hitting the 12-entry cap not because 12 consecutive omissions actually occurred, but because the ring pools unrelated, temporally disjoint, individually-resolved contradiction episodes with no decay (PART 2, hypothesis_bound cluster) — `otto_change1_20260825_session_103B1969_run2` documents six separate episodes (sizes 5,3,1,1,1,1) accumulating to the cap despite each individually resolving via a subsequent matching passage.
- *Falsifying replay test:* PART 2's proposal — report the longest observed run of consecutive expected-but-unadmitted markers under the candidate gate on both Toby and Otto traces — was not directly computed this session; what *was* found is a related, arguably more urgent problem (premature cap-triggered stops from pooled unrelated episodes, not under-sized true-omission coverage).
- *Disposition:* **Unresolved** — the finding's original concern (window too narrow for true omission depth) is unmeasured; a different, replay-confirmed problem with the same constant (ring pooling causing premature terminal stops) has emerged and needs resolution first, since it currently dominates observed behavior.

- *Resolution as built:* Not directly addressed by `templates_replay_spec.py` this session because, per Findings 4/7/5/8's shared discovery, the Grillers capture cannot currently be run through that tool at all (schema incompatibility, confirmed by direct reproduction). `hwt_gate_replay.py` itself (the tool this finding is about) was not touched or re-examined this session — it remains explicitly out of scope as "reference for style/patterns only."
- *Falsifying replay test:* Same schema-adapter work needed for Findings 4/7/5/8 would also let a redesigned candidate correctly window-filter Grillers evidence, closing this alongside those.
- *Disposition:* **Unresolved** — deepened rather than resolved: the underlying capture this finding is about turns out to be entirely unreachable by the current-generation tooling, a strictly larger gap than the original finding identified.

**18. No numeric acceptance threshold exists for false-inclusion or omission rate**
- *Evidence:* No document states a pass/fail bar for either error type; original proposal was (a) near-zero-tolerance false inclusion, checked via a specific known-artifact-class test, and (b) omission bounded by the recovery architecture's own `QUORUM_MAX`=12 slots between confirmed anchors.
- *Attack type:* Doctrine itself — Consequence #10 demands evidence-derived thresholds but supplies none itself.
- *Resolution as built and validated:* Criterion (a) is now directly implemented and operationalized, exactly as proposed: `audit_false_inclusion()` is a standing, independent post-hoc invariant scan built into `templates_replay_spec.py`, and it reports **zero violations across every real session replayed this task** (Toby, Otto CCW, Otto CW, both Change1 sessions — collectively thousands of candidate rows) — no known-artifact-class candidate ever reached `EXPECTED_ADVANCE`/`RESYNC_ADOPTED` in any run. This is strong, real, multi-session confirmation of the doctrine's near-zero-tolerance bar being met by the candidate as built, on the data available. Criterion (b) is directly contradicted by replay evidence: using `QUORUM_MAX`=12 as the omission-tolerance bound was based on the assumption that 12 slots represent 12 genuine consecutive omissions; PART 2's ring-pooling findings show the 12-slot cap is instead frequently exhausted by unrelated, individually-resolved episodes (Toby's own clean-control run hits the cap after only ~133s of otherwise-successful travel, with 11 of 12 entries isolated rather than a genuine streak) — meaning the *bound itself*, not just its size, needs re-derivation before it can serve as an acceptance criterion.
- *Falsifying replay test:* Criterion (a) already run at scale, holds. Criterion (b) would need the ring-clearing-on-confirmed-advance fix (PART 2 proposal) applied first, then re-measurement of the longest *genuine* omission-driven ring accumulation across all five sessions before the 12-slot bound can be re-validated or replaced.
- *Disposition:* **Revise** — criterion (a) is resolved and replay-confirmed at a near-ideal result (zero violations, multi-session); criterion (b) is falsified in its current form and needs the ring-pooling fix before it can be re-evaluated.

## Part 2 — Adversarial Findings Against the Candidate Implementation Itself

17 findings from four adversarial lenses applied to `tools/templates_replay_spec.py` and `docs/TEMPLATES_REPLAY_DESIGN_SPEC.md`, each independently fact-checked (code re-read, evidence re-derived from real captures where applicable). All 17 verdicts: **accurate, not already addressed** (one is a documented-but-unmitigated risk rather than an undiscovered one — noted inline). Grouped by lens.

### Lens: Is the credible-contradiction observation ring actually consumed by recovery, or write-only?

**2.1 — Ring is genuinely consumed, but only reached via the mismatch branch, never on an ordinary matching arrival.** `_try_recovery` (lines 859–929) is real, load-bearing code — it drove Toby's one real `RESYNC_ADOPTED` (offset+2, landing on the physically correct marker 27) and Otto CW's real 9/12-vs-9/12 tied refusal. But it has exactly one call site, reached only from the `CREDIBLE_CONTRADICTION` branch (line 793) when `observed_pol01 != expected_pol01`. Whenever the next credible passage happens to match expected polarity, execution takes `EXPECTED_ADVANCE` (lines 768–779) and never touches the ring at all — pending observations are neither incorporated nor resolved. This is the exact code-level mechanism behind Finding 10's confirmed silent-omission-masking failure. *Proposal:* score offset=0 against offset≥1 on every credible passage, not only mismatches, so a coincidental same-polarity match must still win against live ring evidence before advancing.

**2.2 — Only 2 of ~13 stored observation fields are ever read back by scoring.** `_build_contradiction_observation` stores a rich 13-field record (peak/duration/flux, three PWM-context fields, expected polarity, direction, streak index, rule id, event id) per the operator's explicit finding-#11 contract requirement. `_try_recovery`'s scoring loop reads only `primary_position_at_contradiction` and `observed_polarity` — grep-confirmed no other field is referenced anywhere outside the dict-literal construction site. The rich fields exist purely for the JSON snapshot a human reads on `POSITION_UNRESOLVED`, never for the automated math. A strong (high-peak, long-duration) contradiction counts identically to a marginal one that barely cleared the arrival gate. *Proposal:* weight ring-entry contribution by a credibility factor derived from stored peak/duration/PWM context, or explicitly document equal-weighting as intentional in the design spec (it currently isn't).

**2.3 — Ring pools temporally/spatially disjoint episodes with no decay, because it's cleared only on WIN/reversal/session-reset, never on an intervening confirmed advance.** Confirmed exactly three clear sites (session reset, live reversal, `RESYNC_ADOPTED` win) — `EXPECTED_ADVANCE` never touches it. Real cost, twice: Otto CW's pulse-storm case (13/13 genuine markers correctly advanced while a recurring, unrelated wrong-polarity echo artifact filled the ring to its cap purely by accumulating across the successful advances, forcing a terminal stop 43s into a 59-minute run); Toby's own clean-control run (12 mostly-isolated contradictions, 11 with `contradiction_streak_index=1`, spread across 133s interleaved with 12 confirmed-correct advances, forcing a stop ~15% into a 47-minute run — directly undermining the operator's own stated Toby acceptance bar). *Proposal:* prune ring entries geometrically inconsistent with a newly-confirmed position, or apply recency/count-based decay, on every confirmed advance.

### Lens: Can the liveness monitor false-trigger during legitimate slow movement, or fail to trigger during genuine silence?

**2.4 — `since_prev_max_pwm` is reused for two opposite purposes: fastest-case (arrival gate, correct there) and slowest-case (liveness, wrong there).** A burst-then-creep loco gets a liveness budget sized to its peak speed, not its current crawl, because the field is a monotonic running max reset only on a confirmed push. Confirmed real burst→sustained-creep transitions exist in Otto CCW (three instances, up to 3799ms creep runs). Root cause traces to the design spec itself (§7b explicitly directs reuse of the same `pwm_for_gate` computation as §5.3's arrival-gate model) — an inherited spec-level inconsistency, not a stray coding slip. *Proposal:* track a separate, decaying/windowed recent-PWM statistic for liveness; keep `since_prev_max_pwm` dedicated to the arrival gate where max-since-push is the physically correct quantity.

**2.5 — The below-`PWM_MODEL_VALID_FLOOR` liveness fallback pins to `velocity_mm_s(40)`=56.8mm/s, a speed the doctrine's own Grillers evidence says is already unrealistic under load.** Confirmed the fallback branch never actually fires in any of the five replayed sessions (`since_prev_max_pwm`'s running-max semantics mean it rarely drops below 40 mid-interval) — making the underlying "untested, load-bearing constant" concern *stronger*, since it has never been exercised at all, not merely exercised without incident. No unit test forces this branch. *Proposal:* derive a track-calibrated conservative crawl-speed constant independent of the linear model's boundary value, or flag affected timeouts as lower-confidence; prioritize a schema-adapted Grillers replay (Part 1, Findings 4/5/7/8) before trusting this path.

**2.6 — The liveness record can't distinguish a stalled-but-still-emitting-degraded-magnet-activity window from a genuinely landmark-free one.** `moving_ms_since_prev_push` accumulates only elapsed travel time; `_emit_liveness_timeout_row` reports only `elapsed_ms`/`since_mm` — no count or breakdown of `ARTIFACT`-disposed candidates opened during the window, even though that data already exists in `rows_out`. Real instance: `otto_change1_run1`'s ~65s pre-reversal window packed with dozens of `ARTIFACT_EXCESSIVE_OPEN`/`ARTIFACT_HARD_IMPOSSIBLE` events produces the same bare `elapsed_ms`/`since_mm` record as a window with zero candidate activity at all. Design spec line 371 already promises a "PWM history summary" on this row that the shipped code doesn't include. *Proposal:* have the timeout row report the count/breakdown of `ARTIFACT`-class candidates opened during the window (trivial — track a counter alongside `moving_ms_since_prev_push`, reset together).

**2.7 — Real, fully-steady full-PWM data already grazes the `N_LIVENESS`=3 margin, with little to no headroom even absent degradation.** Independently re-verified: Otto CCW at PWM=90 rock-steady produces 7 real stage-1 warnings in 37 seconds, elapsed 3575–3932ms against a threshold of 3574ms — essentially exactly at the crossing. Extended beyond the original finding: the *same* phenomenon reproduces on Toby's clean-control capture (near-zero contamination, per doctrine field evidence) — 7 stage-1 warnings, same magnitude — which weighs toward the inherited, explicitly-unvalidated `VEL_MODEL_SLOPE`/`INTERCEPT` genuinely overestimating real speed by roughly a consistent factor on both locomotives, rather than admission loss being the cause. *Proposal:* compute the empirical inter-push elapsed-time distribution across all confirmed advances in all five captures, compare against model-predicted intervals, and recalibrate `VEL_MODEL_SLOPE`/`INTERCEPT` (or raise `N_LIVENESS`'s margin) before trusting the threshold as calibrated.

### Lens: Does the omission-hypothesis mechanism bound recovery latency/search, or search indefinitely / silently pick a wrong winner?

**2.8 — ContradictionRing accumulates across successful `EXPECTED_ADVANCE` events, pooling unrelated episodes into one hypothesis test.** Same underlying mechanism as 2.3, filed separately under this lens because the consequence framed here is specifically the hypothesis-search-space contamination (unrelated single-instance artifacts sharing the same 12-slot budget and shared-offset test as a genuine persistent omission), rather than the premature-stop framing in 2.3. Same evidence (Otto CW pulse-storm, Toby clean-control cap-out).

**2.9 — `MIN_SUPPORT`=2 lets `RESYNC_ADOPTED` fire on the thinnest possible ring, on a signal already shown unreliable elsewhere.** Design spec already flags this exact risk explicitly (§7a, lines 349–352 and 453–454/468: "a margin-only test could 'win' on a single data point... the false-lock risk Findings 12 and 13 warn about," marked provisional pending anchor-dense validation) — so the risk itself is documented, not newly discovered. What's new: a concrete mitigation proposal (scale `MIN_SUPPORT` with ring occupancy, or require a second independent corroborating signal such as timing/PWM plausibility before allowing adoption on small rings) not present anywhere in the spec or code. This is the same tension flagged in Part 1, Finding 12.

**2.10 — Below the ring-full cap, an unresolved contradiction has no time or event bound — recovery can dangle indefinitely with no verdict.** Confirmed by control-flow tracing: `_try_recovery` only reaches a terminal disposition via a margin/support win or the 12-entry cap; below the cap, every contradiction just falls through as `CREDIBLE_CONTRADICTION` forever, with `moving_ms_since_prev_push` reset by *any* subsequent confirmed advance (not specifically by resolving the pending doubt) — so ordinary continued navigation on other markers keeps the liveness clock from ever separately escalating the specific outstanding doubt. Reported as inferred-but-untested: no replayed session actually left a sparse, never-resolved ring dangling to end-of-file (all six terminated via cap or win) — the converse failure mode to 2.3/2.8. *Proposal:* bound individual unresolved contradictions independently of ring-fill count (force a verdict, or explicitly expire with a logged reason, after N further confirmed advances or T elapsed ms without corroboration).

**2.11 — `RESYNC_ADOPTED`'s `contributing_ids` over-claims support: it lists the whole ring, not just entries that matched the winning offset.** `contributing_ids = [r['observation_id'] for r in ring]` is built unconditionally from every ring entry, while the actual win condition only requires `leader_s >= MIN_SUPPORT` — a leader can win with, e.g., 5 of 12 entries agreeing, yet all 12 are reported as "contributing." Undermines the audit-trail honesty the operator's Category-3 contract exists to provide. The one real win observed (Toby, ring size 2) happened to have full agreement, so this hasn't yet produced a misleading real report — the risk is structural, confirmed by code, not yet observed misleading a reviewer. *Proposal:* filter `contributing_ids` to entries whose polarity matches the winning offset's hypothesis (the per-entry boolean is already computed in the scoring loop, just not retained), report the full ring separately for context.

### Lens: Is the edge-lobe merge rule's threshold grounded in the replayed companion population, or arbitrary?

**2.12 — `MERGE_WINDOW_MS`/`AMPLITUDE_RATIO_MAX` are still exactly the design doc's provisional guesses, never marked validated even though replay evidence now supports them.** Independently reproduced: 358 real merged companions in Otto CW, amplitude-ratio median=0.219, p99=0.40, max=0.463 — closely matching the doctrine's original ~0.21–0.23 figure the spec's "2x headroom" derivation was built on, and consistent with two independent single-pair values found elsewhere in the replay phase (0.150, 0.198). This is exactly the per-pair Otto CW validation the design spec's own §11 open-item asked for — but the 358-pair sample is inherently self-selected by the threshold being validated (`_merge_eligible` rejects ratio>0.5 by construction), so it doesn't independently prove 0.5 is the right cutoff, only that real companions comfortably clear it. A check for wrongly-excluded near-miss companions (ratio in (0.5,1.0]) found 38 such cases, all independently failing duration/flux floors anyway — reassuring, not proof. *Proposal:* add a validated-by-replay comment citing the real distribution (n=358, median 0.219), mirroring the `ENTRY_COUNTS`/`EXIT_COUNTS` convention already used elsewhere in the file; close design-spec §11 item 3.

**2.13 — `MERGE_WINDOW_MS`=350 sits close to real companion arrival times, not comfortably oversized.** Real dt distribution over the same 358 companions: min=1ms, median=90ms, mean=125.6ms, max=350ms (the ceiling itself), with 7.5% landing in the last 50ms of the window. Checking the finding's own remedy against the data undercuts its stated stakes: all 27 boundary-adjacent (300–350ms) companions independently have duration<40ms and flux<200 — well under the existing duration/flux floors — so shortening the window would just correctly reclassify them as `ARTIFACT`, not lose real evidence or expose risk. Amplitude ratio (the axis the original proposal's remedy names) doesn't discriminate the boundary population either (mean 0.222 vs 0.226 elsewhere) — absolute duration/flux, not a joint dt+ratio function, is what actually separates safe-to-exclude tail companions from risky ones, and that check already exists independently in §3. *Net:* the tension is real and worth resolving, but the practical stakes are lower than the original framing suggested once the duration/flux floors are checked.

**2.14 — `MAX_LOBES`=5 has never been observed truncating a lobe chain during live, position-affecting evaluation.** Confirmed via fresh replay: lobe-count distribution on Otto CW is {2:199, 3:42, 4:17, 5:6} — the cap does bind 6 times in this run, but all 6 occur at t_ms≥363002, entirely inside the frozen `POSITION_UNRESOLVED` tail after the real terminal stop at t=313791 — none occur while the classifier was still live-scoring position. Real 4-lobe chains (one short of the cap) do occur, but all three cited instances are in the `PRE_DECLARATION` window before the manifest's declared start, not inside the genuine live-scoring window itself (a precision correction to the original framing — the underlying claim, that a live 5-lobe chain is physically plausible given adjacent evidence, still holds). No unit test exercises `MAX_LOBES` at all. *Proposal:* construct one targeted synthetic test — inject a same-window, ratio-eligible 5th lobe onto a real 4-lobe chain from the live window — to observe cap-spillover disposition directly rather than relying on the untested design-doc fallback argument.

### Lens: Is PWM-awareness in acquisition/arrival-gate logic actually exercised by real data, or unvalidated code paths?

**2.15 — `PWM_VALIDATED_CEILING`=130 can never fire against any of the four assigned captures.** Max observed PWM: 90 (Toby, Otto CCW), 120 (Otto CW, Change1) — never within 10 counts of 130. Meanwhile `hard_ms` (the one bound *always* active on every candidate) is evaluated at PWM255 regardless of what was actually observed — more than double the highest PWM this session's evidence base ever reached. This is the same underlying gap as Part 1, Finding 2, now with the code-level confirmation that the diagnostic flag meant to surface it is itself untested (zero unit-test coverage forcing it to fire synthetically). *Proposal:* unit-level regression feeding `pwm_for_gate`>130 directly to the `Replay` class; add `max_pwm_for_gate_observed_this_run` as a first-class `report.json` field.

**2.16 — The mechanism explicitly built to close doctrine Findings 4/7 (Grillers sustained-stall) was never run against the purpose-built capture, and that capture is schema-incompatible with the current tool.** Directly reproduced: `grillers.json` fails `Manifest.__init__` outright (key mismatch: `operator_confirmed_start` vs. required `start`); after patching, the underlying CSV (`phys_ch0_raw`/`phys_t_us` schema) silently produces zero candidate rows against `_on_sample`'s required `t_ms`/`phys_raw`/`phys_baseline` fields. Design spec §5.4/§5.7 make an unhedged "directly closes Findings 4 and 7" claim with no provisional caveat, unlike sibling constants in the same document. This is the single largest concrete gap between what the design doc claims and what replay evidence actually supports. *Proposal:* build the schema adapter (map `phys_ch0_raw`/`phys_t_us`→`phys_raw`/`t_ms`, or extend `qt_decode.py`'s older-format path); until then, downgrade the design doc's closure claim to reasoning-only, not replay-validated.

**2.17 — `SUSPEND_PWM_FLOOR`=20 acquisition suppression drops thousands of raw threshold-crossings with zero record, uncharacterized in every replay item.** Confirmed by direct scan: candidates that would have opened under `ENTRY_COUNTS` but were suppressed because `ctl_pwm_actual`≤20 — 0 (Toby), 13,054 (Otto CCW), 3,049 (Otto CW), 7,060 (Otto Change1). None of the seven replay items counts or characterizes this population. Design spec §2.4/§11 item 7 already flags the underlying risk (a genuine passage during low-finalPwm station approach could be suppressed, with no `STATIONS[]` finalPwm table in evidence) as an open item — so the gap itself is doctrine-acknowledged, not hidden; what's missing is empirical characterization of whether the suppressed population clusters near station-approach/creep zones (plausibly real, suppressed passages) or scattered mid-run near-stalls (the frozen-baseline pathology the suppression is designed to catch). *Proposal:* add `suspended_band_crossings_count`/`near_station_stop` diagnostics to the report; correlate against known station/anchor locations in Otto's manifests.
