## Explicit supersession and scope decisions

- Completion-first M0/A05 timing is superseded by M1 appearance. It must not reappear as an implicit quality-completion gate for issuing the tile. Delayed fine-offset availability is a different controller question and already has consequences (F03).
- DART's local opening/polarity qualification, five-pulse/10-mm discrepancy evaluation, IR-based map correction and geographic recovery are not retained justifications. Their active calls were removed; remaining declarations are compatibility only.
- Fixed first-candidate reference is authorized despite the broader architecture's rejection of a permanent golden waveform. This is a scoped experiment, not approval to keep the single pass forever.
- Automatic learning, model transfer, live swap, rollback UI and new sensors are deferred. Their absence must be recorded without making them immediate correction tasks.
- Five **rounds** is not five **pieces**. Separate its missing monitoring/inspection behavior from deferred reference learning and obtain a scope disposition. No new maintenance threshold is selected here.
- Manual priority and current-position operating authority do not automatically disappear when existing stop routines are preserved. H121's objection to completed-visit memory versus `Released`/station locking remains an operating-policy tension; retain short-lived physical brake/dwell protection while David resolves the boundary.
