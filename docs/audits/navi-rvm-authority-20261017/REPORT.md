## Executive summary

**This candidate does not yet have complete behavioral authority under RVM.** It implements a real continuous-waveform alignment kernel; the retired DART Hall-opening recognizer, polarity qualification, nearest-opening selection and five-pulse IR discrepancy evaluator do not execute. It nevertheless combines that kernel with inherited operating decisions that can halt navigation, alter station instructions or override a manual command. Passing the existing header tests does not establish the correctness of those connections.

The largest navigation gaps are the absence of a defensible decision when the expected ensemble never appears, and treating five individually thresholded new-MM qualities as collective loss of pattern. The former can retain old motor instructions indefinitely; the latter gives local quality failures stop authority even though the architecture explicitly rejects five independent pass/fail gates. The candidate also fails its recorded Grillers stationary replay: it emits neither advancement nor the required stall E-STOP. These are separate defects; correcting one does not close the others. ### F01 — No decision when the expected ensemble never yields a token (P1)

**Classification: Contradictory by omission of established loss-of-pattern behavior.** A block with no usable path increments `unreachable_` and returns; insufficient appearance evidence also returns. Neither branch requests a stop or independently evaluates a defensible tile. `badRun_` changes only when an already issued tile's pending quality reaches the next seam. Thus an absent expected ensemble can prevent the very quality assessments needed to reach the five-piece stop. The adapter continues to report reliable position until a separate stop latch fires, and the controller can retain cruise or a previous station command. [RvmRecognizer.h:99–118](https://github.com/ninobur/esp-loco-control/blob/d18fe67e52deda91720be4384cd77c8f53c62cf0/firmware/test-programs/NAVI_RVM/RvmRecognizer.h#L99-L118) [RvmRecognizer.h:140–165](https://github.com/ninobur/esp-loco-control/blob/d18fe67e52deda91720be4384cd77c8f53c62cf0/firmware/test-programs/NAVI_RVM/RvmRecognizer.h#L140-L165) [NaviIntegratedCore.h:129–136](https://github.com/ninobur/esp-loco-control/blob/d18fe67e52deda91720be4384cd77c8f53c62cf0/firmware/test-programs/NAVI_RVM/NaviIntegratedCore.h#L129-L136) [NaviStopOverlay.h:198–204](https://github.com/ninobur/esp-loco-control/blob/d18fe67e52deda91720be4384cd77c8f53c62cf0/firmware/test-programs/NAVI_RVM/NaviStopOverlay.h#L198-L204)

The active inherited control authorities are: command admission, explicit declaration/reset, motor ramps/brake, grade PWM table, station selection and consumed-visit execution, fine-IR position, historical tile timing and its missing-history stop, PWM-zero intervention, startup resource dependencies, electrical protection and warning/state contracts. Some are explicitly retained requirements; their age alone does not justify removal.

Harmless compatibility includes `TargetSpec` polarity, retired event IDs, DART payload fields and constant accessors. `evaluateIrCredibility`, `DartTravelHistory`, `StopArmingPolicy`, the local IR detector/measurement constructor and pulse-event producer are not invoked by this locomotive closure. The `NoHallBySuccessor`, `MissedTarget`, `IrReassessmentFailed` and `IncompleteEvidence` enum names do not themselves prove reachable stop paths. Conversely `NoTimingHistory`, `TimingContextChanged` and the generic stop latch do execute. The ledger identifies the distinction at call sites, not by names.

## Verification and limitations

1. Candidate HEAD and source files were checked before work. The audit lives in an independent checkout based on the exact commit; the original candidate checkout was not edited.
2. Every replay was compiled from the candidate headers and run into this audit's evidence directory. All decision fields match the candidate's committed CSVs. Counts are 21 calibration CW, 10 previously inspected CW, 17 wrap CW, 12 CCW, 7 reference-source multilobe and 0 Grillers. The zero-token Grillers run remains a failed stall behavior.
3. The existing deterministic tests pass. Six additional witnesses confirm the invalid-optics hold, rejected-predecessor mutation, CCW missing-history condition, timed-zero/dwell gap, manual withdrawal and console truncation. Witness success means the described behavior was reproduced, not that the behavior is acceptable.
4. All 38 sources match hashes in the reported ESP32 build. The existing 85% flash/22% static RAM build claim is traceable to those bytes; this audit did not repeat the target compile or measure target runtime. Host timing excludes queues, tasks, radio and motor dynamics.
5. Missing-MM misassignment, real false-stop frequency, trusted-alignment robustness, physical station landing, wrong-source radio occurrence and runtime resource exhaustion are not newly demonstrated field failures. They retain the precise evidence status stated above.
6. Reproduction writes only audit outputs and temporary host binaries: `python3 docs/audits/navi-rvm-authority-20261017/evidence/reproduce.py`. It does not run calibration, modify headers, overwrite candidate test results or connect to hardware.
