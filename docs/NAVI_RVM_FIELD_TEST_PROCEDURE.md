# NAVI_RVM proposed field procedure

This document proposes a later, separately authorized test. **Nothing in this implementation task flashes Otto or changes live services.** The current candidate has a recorded stall-detection failure and unresolved missing-MM and station-timing behavior. It is not ready for unattended AUTO or a claim of safe navigation. Claude's independent review and David's disposition of those findings precede a powered test; passing a compile is insufficient.

## Preparation before any later deployment

1. Review the exact branch commit, [reconciliation](NAVI_RVM_IMPLEMENTATION_RECONCILIATION.md), [test report](NAVI_RVM_BUILD_AND_TEST_REPORT.md) and per-token evidence. Resolve the recorded stall failure and agree how to handle a missing expected ensemble. Do not retune a threshold during a run to force a successful sequence.
2. Identify the exact currently installed, operator-accepted rollback image and its source/build manifest. Preserve its binary, flash layout, credentials, pairing information and identity. The EWO baseline manifest included here identifies one historical build but its local source differs in five files and it is not represented as a known-good rollback release. If the installed/accepted image cannot be identified, defer deployment.
3. Compile the approved candidate for `esp32:esp32:esp32`, Otto 9950011, with the existing private credentials. Record commit, ELF/binary hashes, toolchain and model version. Obtain David's separate flashing authorization. Use the established deployment procedure only after that authorization; this document deliberately performs no deployment.
4. Use only Otto, with other locomotives excluded from the test area. Keep the existing operator E-STOP available and verify it independently of navigation, initially with drive restrained using the established bench procedure. Do not rely on the candidate's stall detector as the sole stopping protection.
5. Capture the existing console, MQTT telemetry and NSR Hall/IR/PWM recording streams. Confirm the boot banner says `NAVI_RVM`, `RVM_FIXED_REFERENCE_NOT_FIELD_ACCEPTED`, Otto 9950011. Check allocation/queue faults and `diag/resource` heap/stack/timing output before motion. No Pi service or dashboard modification is required.

## Initial bounded single-locomotive test

1. Begin **CW**, because the inherited prescribed timing table is CW only. Use an unobstructed, attended section and the established operator-selected settings. Place Hall clear of a magnet in the actual declared interval. Set direction and declare location through the existing console while actual and commanded PWM are zero. Confirm current MM and expected successor immediately.
2. First observe a short manually controlled traverse with AUTO unenrolled. Verify manual throttle/stop behavior and independent E-STOP. Record the physical MM sequence independently (operator observation plus synchronized video if available). The recognizer may observe manual travel; its ordinary stop/quality paths must not be mistaken for manual authorization changes. Stop promptly on unexpected navigation withdrawal or resource loss.
3. For every tile, compare the live `diag/rvm_decision` / `[RVM]` row with independent observation. Check boot/run/ordinal uniqueness, expected and committed MM, source serial/time bounds, commit time and consumption lag. `EXPECTED_PIECE_APPEARED` or `COUNTED_PROVISIONALLY_ATYPICAL` is a tile instruction; `FINAL_COHERENT` or `FINAL_ATYPICAL_NO_REANCHOR` is the later assessment of the **same ordinal**, not another tile. Do not judge a run by total count alone.
4. Include a known split/multilobe ensemble. Check that a trailing flat lobe does not create another tile. Monitor quality, trusted MM and mismatch streak; do not physically alter a magnet or inject a sensor fault during the initial run.
5. Perform an ordinary commanded STOP within a developing ensemble, leave the locomotive powered and undisturbed, then GO under the existing controls. Confirm no stopped-time advancement/rebaseline and no duplicate ordinal on resumption. An actual PWM-zero wheel-pulse warning requires inspection/redeclaration; do not silently clear it as “coasting.”
6. After short manual observations are accepted, David may authorize a bounded AUTO/station trial. Use the existing centers 015/063/108/157 unchanged. Independently measure appearance-token time, physical sensor/body position, transition between timing fallback and fine IR, braking, dwell and landing position. Begin with one station and an available manual stop, then extend only on the operator's judgment. The roughly one-MM body/sensor offset does not establish that changed trigger timing is acceptable.
7. Test a different start declaration separately, at rest. CW wraparound and later CCW recognition/stop behavior require their own evidence. CCW station automation is not accepted here: absent timing history may cause controlled STOP. Do not reverse within a run; stop and redeclare after a direction change.

## Stop conditions and evidence

Use the existing E-STOP for an actual obstruction, uncontrolled movement or unsafe approach. A missing/extra/early physical tile, unexplained persistent phase/quality discrepancy, significant observation loss, allocation failure, reset, or station command inconsistent with observation ends the bounded test. Record the first discrepancy and surrounding original samples; do not edit the map, advance the declared MM to conceal it, or certify the rest of the lap by final count.

Expected implemented protection reasons are `LOSS_OF_PATTERN_FIVE_NEW_PIECES` (controlled stop) and `LOSS_OF_PROGRESSION_ESTOP` (detected stall). The current recorded stall failure means absence of the latter is **not** evidence that the locomotive is moving. After either protective stop, inspect and redeclare before further navigation. Ordinary STOP/GO requires neither a model reset nor redeclaration when the locomotive has remained powered and unmoved.

Preserve commit/build/model hashes, boot ID, declaration, command log, every available decision and final-quality row, resource counters, raw NSR/IR records, independent physical annotations and observed landing positions. A dropped decision log makes the interval incomplete; the final count cannot repair that evidence.

## Rollback

On a failed test, stop Otto using the existing controls and keep it stopped. Archive the candidate's raw evidence first. With David's explicit deployment authorization, restore the previously identified operator-accepted image through the established flashing procedure. Verify its boot identity, locomotive ID and normal manual/E-STOP behavior before returning to operation; restore only any configuration actually changed during the authorized test. This task changed none. Do not reset or force-push repository history: the candidate branch and its failure evidence remain available for review.
