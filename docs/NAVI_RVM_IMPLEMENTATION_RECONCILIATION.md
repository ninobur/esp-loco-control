## Mechanism actually implemented

[`RvmRecognizer.h`](../firmware/test-programs/NAVI_RVM/RvmRecognizer.h), `Recognizer::observe`, advances a monotone dynamic-programming frontier over **one expected map interval**, with 5 mm cells, at most 256 states, and two fixed arrays. Each state carries accumulated alignment cost, appearance contrast, and new-MM quality sufficient statistics. Sliding the storage window retains overlapping states and their relative accumulated costs; a token does not reseed the frontier at the new MM. There is no candidate search across 171 geographic identities.

Live Hall samples are averaged in disjoint causal 20 ms blocks. A fixed reference cell supplies a mean and soft scale. The residual loss is Huber-like, capped at eight; gross incompatible excursions therefore have bounded influence. PWM history and eligible IR speed contribute small bounded transition costs. A zero-displacement transition is always allowed. Neither motion term contributes to magnetic appearance evidence.

After the reference's between-MM seam, each path integrates the difference between explaining the same observed Hall value as unchanged baseline and explaining it with the expected continuous waveform. The baseline alternative remains numerically evaluable even when alignment states are pruned. The frozen empirical appearance threshold produces one geographic token. This is a numeric waveform predicate, not an ADC crossing, lobe width, polarity veto, peak crossing or fixed number of confirmation samples.

The token is irreversible. Its new-MM quality continues through the trailing context until the next reference seam; that generates a separate **quality row with the same ordinal**, never a second tile. Five consecutive atypical final assessments request controlled STOP. Atypical pieces do not promote trusted context. Two consecutive coherent final assessments permit promotion. This two-assessment promotion policy, bounded search geometry, residual scale and stall durations are engineering choices in this candidate, not established operating decisions.

## Independent review focus

Evaluate whether retaining minimum-cost path statistics while moving a local window sufficiently preserves trusted whole-pattern context, especially through a malformed or missing MM. Audit the empirically chosen appearance contrast against broad leading lobes: it must not become a renamed event detector. Review the stall failure, incomplete missing-MM handling, single-pass corridor and changed fine-position origin before treating this as an operational navigator. The [build report](NAVI_RVM_BUILD_AND_TEST_REPORT.md) contains measurements and reproducible evidence; the [field procedure](NAVI_RVM_FIELD_TEST_PROCEDURE.md) is a proposal for later authorization, not permission to flash.
