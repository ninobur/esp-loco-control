

Summary of CTO / Bubble Design Discussion
Core Architecture Principles
Mile Marker (MM) System Foundation: Uses precise, measured positions (Hall sensors + DNA map) for controlled speed profiles, glidepaths, stops, and separation. This eliminates unpredictable coasting based on topography or momentum.
Reusable Station Stop Template: A single flexible template supports multiple behaviors:
Platform Stop (lead train/normal service): Glide from cruise → 15 pKPH at M−5 → traverse at 15 → 10 at M+1 → 5 at M+2 → final ramp to stop slightly beyond M+2.
Holding Stop (trailing/follower): Earlier glide start (e.g., M−12 or adjusted), reaches 15 pKPH earlier, then enters final 10/5/stop sequence at a shifted target (e.g., M−5 or dynamically adjusted).
Glidepath & Control: Linear pKPH reductions (e.g., ~5–7 pKPH per interval), measured-speed governor, gradual PWM changes. No fixed PWM jumps. Final ramp (e.g., ~150–300 ms/PWM) is tunable. ESP driver handles execution; higher logic decides why/where.
Dwell: Configurable/randomized (e.g., min/max range like 12–20s normal, shorter 0–10s for lead in bubble). Selected on stop.
Two-Train “Bubble” Concept
Physical Moving Envelope: Not a fixed block. Roughly:
2 ft ahead of lead loco Hall sensor.
Lead loco + consist.
Trailing loco + consist.
5 ft behind trailing loco Hall sensor.
Internal Safety: Trailing loco Hall sensor maintains ~5 MM margin behind lead loco Hall sensor (separate from outer envelope). Prevents overlap even as bubble “stretches/compresses like a slinky.”
Operation:
Lead stops at platform (M+2), waits for trailing confirmation at hold point.
Lead does short randomized dwell (0–10s), then departs.
Trailing (at hold) waits for adequate moving separation, then restarts toward 15 pKPH, traverses station, and does its own platform stop.
Follower can depart hold sooner than old fixed-block rules (once dwell + separation allows), following a moving authority limit behind the lead.
Broadcasts & Decisions: Minimal peer data via ESP-NOW (position, direction, speed, state, timestamp). Bubble layer sits above navigation/governor/station logic. Dispatcher/coordinator handles authority.
Consist & Flexibility Enhancements (Future-Proofing)
Dynamic Consist/Formation Offsets: Dispatcher supplies per-train details (standard/long/short consist, offsets like +1/+2 or -1). Adjusts:
Protected rear boundary (rear of lead consist + clearance).
Follower holding stop target (moves backward for longer consists).
Allows mixing train lengths without hard-coded assumptions. Station template uses supplied stop targets rather than fixed offsets from center.
Development Sequence
Station Stop v1: Single loco, single test stop (Arches). Prove full cycle (glide → stop → dwell → reliable restart). No bubble yet.
Station Service v2: Apply template to multiple stations, scheduled stops, randomized dwells, etc.
CTO/Bubble v2: Add peer reporting, holding stops, moving authority, handshakes, and (later) consist offsets. Preserve R21 single-loco station logic unchanged.
Key Agreements & Notes
Bubble/role logic decides where/why to slow/stop/hold; motor control remains consistent (measured glide + ramp).
Prioritize clean separation of concerns: Navigation, Station Controller, Governor, Bubble/Dispatcher layer.
Supports future extensions (e.g., third train, opposite-direction bubbles at locations like Arches/Bamboo).
First implementation keeps it minimal and testable; reserve slots in data structures (e.g., StopPlan.stopTarget) for dynamic elements.
This builds on proven R21 station behavior while enabling reliable, safe multi-train “bubble” operations that feel more prototype-like. The discussion emphasizes controlled, predictable movement over legacy coasting/fixed blocks.
