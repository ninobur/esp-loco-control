## What this run showed

1. **Fallback-to-preferred handoff (the principal result).** Declaration → no MM
   reference → MM048 accepted Hall-only → the MM048 strike, with an IR point
   86 ms away in epoch 2, became the MM/IR synchronization → MM047/046/045
   judged by same-epoch IR distance against cumulative mapped distance, all
   well inside ±15%. First field use of the 20Q3 Epoch-based window path.
2. **70-count opening.** Detections opened at departures of 70–72 counts from the
   lock; magnet peaks 204–221 counts.
3. **Opening polarity only.** All four opening poles match the map. Every
   `nav/hall_window` record agreed with the opening (`agrees_with_opening:1`,
   `window_drops:0`, `nav_authority:"NONE"`).
4. **No sans-MM advance, correctly.** The stop at 0.61D is short of MM044's
   1.15D, and epoch 2 ended at the stop. No `nav/sans_mm` messages.
5. **Hall speed kept with age (ruling 8).** Hall-derived 69, 74, 66 mm/s agree
   with IR 64–79 mm/s. After the stop, 66 mm/s was held while `est_age_ms`
   rose 369 → 14,378 ms.
6. **IR at the stop (observed TX behavior).** Epoch 2 ended 08:53:00.2 with
   `INADEQUATE_CONTRAST` at PWM 19 while still decelerating (IR 19.3 mm/s the
   second before). NAVI displayed STOPPED from 08:53:10. While standing, IR
   alternated `REACQUIRING`/`INADEQUATE_CONTRAST`, including a one-pulse
   epoch 3; no navigation effect.
7. **Clean transport.** Final loopstat: 4 Hall events, all drop counters 0.
   No corrections; recovery in WARMUP (4 positions).

## Observations recorded, not acted on

- **X22R baseline never re-locked (Sam: "deserves attention eventually").**
  All four baseline decisions were `reject:2` (`Cadence`): X22 re-locks only
  when an interval was entered within `cadenceMaxMs` = 3000 ms, and at about
  70 mm/s each interval took 4.0–4.5 s. The run operated throughout, and
  successfully, on the boot lock of 1828 (age about 194 s at the end). The
  locked-baseline principle is not in question. Per the track-evidence rule,
  one run is recorded, not changed; watch whether it recurs at cruise speed.
- **Misleading `expected` field** in `mm/marker` / `state/nav`: it shows the
  pole of the next target after the advance, not of the MM judged (e.g. MM048
  `obs:N expected:S` while correctly `CONFIRMED`). Telemetry semantics only;
  identical in the `57d34d6` base. Correct later.
- `telem/speed` published "0" at the first event: the never-measured initial
  value. The status line's `est_age_ms:null` disambiguates it; `telem/speed`
  alone does not.
- The status line's `ir_interval` stayed `MM_REFERENCED_EPOCH` after epoch 2
  ended: it reports the basis of the last Hall judgment, not live IR state.
  `diag/ir_health` is the live source.
