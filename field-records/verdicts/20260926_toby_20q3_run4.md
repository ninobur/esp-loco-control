## Anomalies and findings for review (recorded; no change proposed here)

1. **No stall recognition with power applied.** Twice, IR showed no motion
   and no Hall event arrived while PWM stayed applied:
   - 12.7 s at PWM 90 in AUTO, ended only by the operator's E-stop;
   - more than 14 s at PWM 75 in manual, ended by the power cut.

   The status `moving` field went to null and the IR epoch ended
   (`INADEQUATE_CONTRAST`), but no warning, stop or AUTO withdrawal
   followed. This is a safety observation about current behavior. Whether
   NAVI or the control layer should act on it is a design question for
   David.
2. **Bamboo stop almost on the next magnet (MM156).** The stop at off 0 was
   about 0.97 D past the station MM. The restart was handled correctly.
   Stopping on or next to a magnet is the condition behind run 2 #30 and
   run 3's reversal error. Recorded for the station-stop design, not a
   navigation error here.
3. **`NO_IR_POINT` inside an active epoch (#151, 09:59:36.5).** Epoch 18
   stayed active and HEALTHY, but the IR point nearest the strike was 151 ms
   away (`ir_reason:"CLOCK_UNCERTAIN"`, `ir_alignment_us:-151418`, IR frame
   age 148 ms). #151 was therefore accepted Hall-only, and #152 had no MM
   reference. Two MMs lost IR judgment through IR frame timing, not epoch
   loss. No navigation effect.
4. **The two obstruction stops show different current signatures:** falling
   (0.43 → 0.25 A at PWM 90) and rising (to 0.53 A at PWM 75). Recorded only.
5. **X22R baseline:** 110 locks accepted. There were 6 `Cadence` rejects,
   all at a restart after a stop or declaration, and 1 `Stopped` (the
   E-stop). At these cruise intervals the per-interval lock works, as in
   run 3.
6. Every `nav/hall_window` record agreed with the opening (117 of 117,
   `window_drops:0`, `nav_authority:"NONE"`).
