# Source-only morphology review

Frozen before evaluation predictions were generated. Reviewer: Codex, one analyst; not an independent operator or surveyed magnetic-field boundary measurement. Input: the six `source_lobes_*.png` and four `source_focus_*.png` source-only panels, the prior 14 recurrent undershoot interpretations, raw 20-ms ADC and the eligibility mask. No recognition output was read to select landmarks or uncertainty ranges.

Each source panel contains three visually distinct principal fields. The middle field in the six lobe panels has a previously reviewed within-ensemble undershoot. The four focus panels contain repeated MM050–MM052 hypotheses (one from boot A, two CW and one CCW from boot B). Repeated undershoot/recovery morphology supports grouping; geographic numbers remain NAVI hypotheses. The CSV records 30 source structures, their principal, leading feature, secondary feature and recovery landmarks, and deliberately nonpoint start/end ranges. The secondary feature of a negative principal can be a small positive overshoot. Ranges reflect ambiguity of gentle source curves; they are not ADC admission thresholds and are never fed into the recognizer.

One case's third field (MM088 hypothesis near 2578.83 s) has excluded observations in its trailing recovery. The third focus_B1 field also crosses an excluded trailing-recovery interval. Mark each affected whole field and incident unit censored rather than bridge the gap. All other coverage is checked programmatically, not assumed from the plot.

A reviewed complete-field success requires one closed MM state containing all four source landmarks, no neighboring principal, and start and end inside the recorded uncertainty ranges. A lobe-grouping result alone requires the principal, secondary feature and recovery to share one closed MM state; it is weaker than complete recognition. A duplicate is multiple MM states intersecting the confident ownership interval from earliest through latest feature. A reviewed baseline core runs from the preceding latest plausible return (`end_hi_s`) to the following earliest plausible departure (`start_lo_s`). Report every MM intrusion into those cores; a contained false MM is a stricter subset. Explicit BL coverage and corresponding boundary errors are separately reported. A complete MM–BL–MM unit requires two reviewed complete fields and one explicit BL between them containing the core without another MM. Consecutive units must reuse the identical central MM ID.

Boundary error is distance to the adjudicated uncertainty interval (zero anywhere inside), with error to the range midpoint also available; midpoint uncertainty is induced by start/end ranges. This cannot establish railway-wide accuracy. The other source openings remain unresolved for physical completeness, even if their association is unambiguous. No later evaluation disagreement changes this review.
